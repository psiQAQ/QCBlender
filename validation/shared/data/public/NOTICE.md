# Public tutorial samples

Current dataset: Gaussian-v2, public samples v4. Owned calculation data: CC BY 4.0; attribution QCBlender contributors. P04 contains 53 native Gaussian GS2 frames (TS27), generated by 52 segmented Forward/Reverse then Downhill integrations. Both endpoints are frequency-checked at their final accepted coordinates, without endpoint relaxation frames. Conventional single-job early termination remains a failed diagnostic; this data is native segmented continuation. P06 takes coordinate-only XYZ frames from steps 1/27/53.

P01/P02/P03/P05 and the P06 water-dimer XYZ retain Gaussian-v1 scientific bytes, methods, bases, 5D and explicit DFT grid settings. P02 is an owned two-job optimization/frequency/NBO calculation; P05 fragments retain their optimized-complex geometries. Historical Gaussian-v1 (69 frames, including endpoint relaxation), the v3 package, and the Gaussian-native-IRC-v1 supplement retain their identities. Native FCHK files are produced by Gaussian formchk.

Gaussian 16 Revision C.01 is the wavefunction producer. Multiwfn 2026.9.20 produces IGMH/IRI, ESP/AIM and NOCV analyses. Cite Tian Lu, Feiwu Chen, J. Comput. Chem. 33, 580 (2012), DOI:10.1002/jcc.22885; Tian Lu, J. Chem. Phys. 161, 082503 (2024), DOI:10.1063/5.0216272.

Gaussian-v1 IRC integration and endpoint relaxation use geomeTRIC 1.1.1; cite L.-P. Wang and C. Song, J. Chem. Phys. 144, 214108 (2016), DOI:10.1063/1.4952956.

NOCV energies use the actual complex KS reconstruction and retain the Multiwfn approximation; they are not evaluated using F_TS. No basis-set or scientific grid-convergence claim is made. QCBlender display grids default to 0.2 angstrom; externally generated analysis grids are recorded separately in the current manifest. Original program outputs retain their scientific values; public copies omit machine execution details.

Legacy-v2 inputs and their frozen manifest remain historical data. Their previous scientific and GUI evidence does not qualify this dataset. The old restricted P02 input is not included.
